- Require a dedicated OTC fill: one recovery OTC, exact WETH funding and consumption, and exact sdCRV delivery to BotMarket. Verify source claims, withdrawal, bridge receipts, deposit event, OTC record and fill receipts. Reject mixed OTC fills, other spent tokens and pre-existing CRV/sdCRV balances. The legacy job's 0.1% WETH retention is not suitable for this fill.
- Remove that fill transaction and OTC from ordinary weekly reports. A matching OTC without a completed recovery ledger stops OTC reporting.
- Split actual settled sdCRV across the source epochs in proportion to USDC amounts. Reuse the original proposals' direct-voter, auto-voter and delegation logic; normalize wallet weights using integer arithmetic and deterministic remainder allocation.
- Add wallet amounts as exact wei to the target Merkle. Apply the recovery only in its settlement period; reruns rebuild the same addition, and later periods use normal claim-aware carry. Published historical trees are unchanged. The legacy distributor uses unclaimed carry, not cumulative entitlements.
